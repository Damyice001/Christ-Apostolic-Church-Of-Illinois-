Who we are

A family rooted in faith & love

Christ Apostolic Church (CAC) Miracle Centre is a vibrant, Spirit-filled congregation that believes in the full gospel of Jesus Christ — His power to save, heal, deliver, and transform every life.

We are part of the Christ Apostolic Church, a movement rooted in the Aladura revival of faith — a tradition of fervent prayer, dependence on the Holy Spirit, and unwavering commitment to the Word of God.

At Miracle Centre, sound biblical doctrine, passionate worship, and genuine community come together. No matter who you are or where you have been, you are welcome here.

Our doctrine

What we believe

The Holy Scriptures

The Bible is the inspired, infallible Word of God and the supreme authority for all faith and conduct.

Salvation in Christ

Salvation is by grace through faith in Jesus Christ alone — His death, burial, and resurrection.

The Holy Spirit

We believe in the baptism of the Holy Spirit with evidence, and the gifts of the Spirit active today.

Divine Healing

God still heals today. Healing of body, mind, and spirit is part of the finished work of Christ.

Our Pastor

Spiritual Leader · Christ Apostolic Church of Illinois

Dr. Joshua Owoeye

Dr. Joshua Owoeye is the Spiritual Leader of Christ Apostolic Church of Illinois and a committed minister of the full gospel of Jesus Christ. With decades of ministry experience, his life's work has been teaching the whole counsel of God's Word — with conviction, clarity, and pastoral compassion.

His passion is the making of true disciples: believers who are not just spiritually enthusiastic, but deeply grounded in Scripture, holy in character, and effective in service to God and neighbour.

Dr. Owoeye is also a prolific author and teacher whose writings on character, holiness, sin, leadership, and church accountability continue to impact lives across Nigeria and in the diaspora.
